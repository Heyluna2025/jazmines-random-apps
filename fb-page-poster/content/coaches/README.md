# Coach Spotlight series

One 4-slide post per coach (`coach-<name>.json`): photo and title, what they built with AI, their tip for beginners, and a "Learn with Coach X" call to action pointing to aibuilderscamp.ph. Every `[[...]]` is filled from the coach's answers below before posting.

## Form to send each coach

Hi Coach! We're featuring you in our **Coach Spotlight** series on the AI Empowered Club Facebook Page and Instagram. Please send:

1. **Photo:** a clear, well-lit headshot or portrait (face and shoulders), the higher resolution the better.
2. **Name as shown:** e.g. "Coach Ann" (add your full name if you'd like it in the caption).
3. **Title, 2–4 words:** e.g. "Automation Coach", "AI Content Coach".
4. **Known for, one line:** what people come to you for.
5. **Something you built with AI:** the problem, what you built, and the tool you used (1–2 lines). A screenshot is a bonus.
6. **Your tip for AI beginners:** one sentence or your favourite line.
7. **Your Instagram or Facebook handle** to tag (optional).
8. **Your OK** for us to post your photo and these details publicly on Facebook and Instagram.

Please don't include income or earnings claims, or other people's names or screenshots without their permission.
